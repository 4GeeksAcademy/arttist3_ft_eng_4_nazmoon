About Me

Background

Born and raised in Chicago, Nazmoon discovered her love for music at an early age. Growing up surrounded by the rich traditions of gospel, jazz, and soul, she developed a sound that is both timeless and refreshingly modern.

After graduating from the Berklee College of Music, she spent years honing her craft in local venues before breaking onto the national scene.

Musical Style & Interests

Nazmoon's music is a tapestry woven from R&B, soul, and subtle electronic textures. Her lyrics explore themes of identity, resilience, love, and belonging — drawing listeners into an intimate sonic experience.

Influenced by artists like Erika Badu, Jeff Buckley, and Hiatus Kaiyote, she brings a fresh perspective to modern soul music.

Career

2018

Debut EP — First Light

Released independently, First Light garnered over 500K streams on Spotify within its first month, earning Nazmoon a spot on several curated New Artist playlists.

2020

National Tour Opener

Opened for Grammy-nominated artist Jade Wind on her 20-city US tour, performing to sold-out audiences across the country.

2022

Single — "Golden Hour"

The single reached #12 on the Billboard Adult R&B chart and was featured in major publications including NPR Music and Billboard.

2024

Debut Album — Rooted

Her debut full-length album dropped in Spring 2024, debuting at #4 on the iTunes R&B chart and earning a nomination for Best New R&B Artist at the Soul Music Awards.

2025

Headlining Tour & Festival Appearances

Completed her first headlining tour across North America and performed at major festivals including Lollapalooza and Essence Festival.
